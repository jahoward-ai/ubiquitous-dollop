Annual cycle

Prepare in the fall. Deliver in the spring.

Fall

Source and evaluate potential clients, define a feasible scope and timeline, identify information needs, prepare project documents, and assemble the team.

Spring

Conduct research and analysis, communicate with the client, review work as a team, develop recommendations, and close the engagement.
