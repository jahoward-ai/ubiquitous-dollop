Campus Lounge Modernization

Quill supported a campus-services team considering changes to a shared lounge.

Challenge

Understand how students used the space, what they valued, and what needs a future design should address.

Approach

Analyze student survey responses, compare the roles of different campus spaces, and synthesize recurring preferences and tradeoffs.

Advisory contribution

Organize the research into clear considerations the client could use when evaluating possible changes.

Client identity, location, detailed survey findings, recommendations, and investment figures remain confidential.
