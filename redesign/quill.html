The Quill year

Prepare in the fall. Deliver in the spring.

Each engagement runs across the academic year, so teams have time to scope the question properly before they start the analysis.

Fall Set up

Find and scope the project

Source and evaluate potential clients, define a feasible scope and timeline, identify information needs, prepare project documents, and assemble the team.

Spring Deliver

Do the work and present it

Conduct research and analysis, communicate with the client, review work as a team, develop recommendations, and close the engagement.

For students

Do the work between meetings, and improve it together.

Quill participants contribute to a real client project through individual work, weekly team meetings, review, and integration into a final deliverable.

Weekly team meetings

Discuss findings, test interpretations, review progress, coordinate tasks, and decide next steps.

Work between meetings

Complete assigned research, analysis, writing, or presentation work and arrive prepared to explain it.

Feedback and integration

Review teammates’ contributions, respond to feedback, and combine separate work into one coherent client deliverable.

Selective staffing

Project needs determine the team.

Quill participation is separate from General Membership, Active Membership, Partnership, and other leadership titles. Staffing considers project needs, capability, professionalism, reliability, commitment, and available supervision.

Executive Board members receive primary consideration under the mandate, and Active Members may also be selected. No status guarantees a place on an engagement.

What we expect

Be prepared, reliable, and careful with information.

  • Meet agreed deadlines and communicate early when circumstances change.
  • Attend prepared and contribute constructively.
  • Use evidence carefully and make limitations visible.
  • Protect client information and follow the project’s confidentiality requirements.
  • Treat feedback as part of the work.

For organizations

Research and analysis to help you make a decision.

A potential engagement starts with a focused question and an exploratory conversation. Quill provides accepted work at no charge. It is not a licensed professional-services firm, and your organization remains responsible for its decisions.

What makes a good fit

A defined decision

You can explain the question and how you expect to use the work.

Feasible access

The team can get the information the agreed scope reasonably needs.

An active counterpart

A named contact answers questions, reviews progress, and gives timely feedback.

How it works

  1. Explore

    Discuss your organization, the question, the decision, and timing.

  2. Assess and scope

    Review feasibility, staffing, information needs, confidentiality, conflicts, and fit.

  3. Research and analyze

    Gather agreed inputs, test interpretations, and review progress with you.

  4. Present

    Share findings, limitations, and recommendations for you to consider.

Spring 2026 Campus services

Campus Lounge Modernization

Quill helped a campus-services team understand how students used a shared lounge, what they valued, and what a future design should address. The team analyzed survey responses, compared the roles of different campus spaces, and organized the findings into considerations the client could use when evaluating changes.

Spring 2026 Food and beverage

Food-and-Beverage Demand Strategy

Quill helped a food-and-beverage operator get a clearer view of customer demand. The team combined available research, examined demand patterns, tested interpretations and scenarios, and presented strategic considerations for the client’s own decision process. No implemented result is claimed.
