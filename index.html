Why Hamilton

Hamilton’s liberal arts education builds habits students can apply in consulting.

Writing-intensive courses require students to make an argument and support it. Seminars develop careful listening and discussion. Quantitative and qualitative work teach students to test evidence from more than one angle.

Across classrooms, teams, clubs, and campus leadership, students learn to take ownership, collaborate, and communicate clearly—habits that matter when the question is ambiguous and the answer must be useful.
