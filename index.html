Why Hamilton

Hamilton students arrive with the habits consulting runs on.

Clients hire consultants to make sense of messy questions and explain the answer clearly. Hamilton trains students to do exactly that, long before their first case interview.

  • Writing-intensive coursesMake an argument and back it with evidence, the core of every recommendation.
  • Seminar discussionListen closely, ask sharper questions, and work through competing views.
  • The open curriculumConnect ideas across disciplines and test a problem from more than one angle.
  • Teams, clubs, and athleticsTake ownership, collaborate, and speak directly when it matters.
