Questions

Common questions.

Do I need consulting experience?
No. General Membership is open to interested Hamilton students, and HCG’s overview sessions start with the basics.
Do I have to apply?
Not for General Membership. Partnership has an application, and Quill has its own selection process.
What counts toward Active Membership?
Participating in two of three designated core events in a semester.
How do I become a Partner?
Partnership is a leadership path involving Executive Board service and portfolio ownership. Open the Partnership application.
How is Quill different from HCG?
The Group teaches consulting; Quill practices consulting. Quill participation is selective and separate from membership. See how it works.
Where do I get case interview help?
Ask about casing office hours or request the case book, case library, or a practice case using the form below.
