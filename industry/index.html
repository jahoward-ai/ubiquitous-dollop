Understanding consulting

How a client engagement works

Management consultants are external advisors who help organizations understand difficult questions and consider possible actions. An engagement is a client project with a defined question, scope, timeline, team, and set of deliverables.

Teams clarify the question, break it into manageable parts, gather and analyze evidence, discuss findings with the client, and present recommendations. Consultants advise; the client remains responsible for decisions and implementation.

Firms & careers

Different firms organize the work differently

Broad professional-services firms, strategy firms, specialist boutiques, and mission-focused organizations can differ in their clients, project mix, office model, staffing, and career paths. Practice areas may focus on a function such as operations or a sector such as healthcare.

Entry roles may be generalist or specialist. Experience can lead to firm leadership, corporate strategy, investing, startups, public service, graduate study, and other paths.

Industries & challenges

The subject changes; the problem-solving discipline carries across

Consultants may work with businesses, nonprofits, public institutions, or other organizations. Questions can involve growth, pricing, operations, organization design, customer experience, digital change, or due diligence.

  • What is driving a change in demand?
  • How could an organization improve an operation?
  • Which opportunity should a team pursue?
  • What evidence should inform an investment or partnership?
