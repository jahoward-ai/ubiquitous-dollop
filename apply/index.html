Ways to participate

Choose the level that fits.

General Membership

Open to interested students, with no attendance obligation. Attend open events, use public resources, and receive programming updates.

Join general programming

Active Membership

Participate in two of three designated core events in a semester. The mandate also allows the Group Managing Partner to recognize an equivalent contribution.

Partnership

A leadership path involving Executive Board service and portfolio ownership. The existing application opens in Google Forms.

Open Partnership application

Quill

Selective participation in client projects. Membership or leadership does not guarantee staffing; selection depends on project needs and the approved operating criteria.

Explore Quill participation

Not sure where to begin?

General programming is the simplest first step.

Start by learning about consulting, attending confirmed programming, and using HCG’s preparation resources. You can consider Active Membership, Partnership, or Quill later.
