Questions

Common questions about joining.

Do I need consulting experience to join?

No. General Membership is open to interested Hamilton students, and HCG’s overview sessions start with the basics of what consultants do.

Do I have to apply?

Not for General Membership. Send a request to join general programming and you will get updates about open events. Partnership has an application; Quill has its own selection process.

What counts toward Active Membership?

Participating in two of three designated core events in a semester. The Events page notes which sessions are designated.

How do I become a Partner?

Partnership is a leadership path involving Executive Board service and portfolio ownership. Open the Partnership application.

How is Quill different from HCG?

The Group teaches consulting; Quill practices consulting. Quill participation is selective and separate from membership. See how Quill participation works.

Where can I get help preparing for case interviews?

Ask about casing office hours, or request the HCG case book, case library, or a practice case from the Recruiting & Case Prep page.
